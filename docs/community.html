Reporting a bug well

A good report gets fixed faster. Include:

  • Your device and its iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The game and what you were doing.
  • What happened and what you expected.
  • Whether JIT was on and how much memory the limit was raised to.

EspressU

A free, open-source Wii U emulator for iPad and iPhone.

EspressU is built on Cemu and based on MuffinEMU by Void.

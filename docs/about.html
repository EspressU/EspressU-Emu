About and credits

What EspressU is built on, and how it is licensed.

Built on

EspressU is built on Cemu, the Wii U emulator. It is based on MuffinEMU by Void.

Licence

EspressU is free and open source under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. The copyright notices in the source files stay in place. Libraries and their licences are listed in the app under Settings, About, Open-source licences.

Not affiliated with Nintendo

Wii U is a trademark of Nintendo. EspressU is not affiliated with Nintendo, and includes no firmware, keys or games. Use only games you legally own and dumped yourself.

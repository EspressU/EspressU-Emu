Questions

Short answers to what people ask first.

Do I need JIT?

JIT is what makes games run well. Without it the much slower interpreter runs. JIT needs iOS 17.4 or later.

Does EspressU include games, keys or firmware?

No. You need your own dumps from your own Wii U. EspressU can load .wua files, decrypted folders, or encrypted ones with your own keys.txt.

Why does a game close on its own?

Most games run out of memory unless the limit is raised. Install GetMoreRam and use it on EspressU.

How do I install it?

Download the unsigned IPA from the Releases page and sideload it with SideStore or AltStore. Developer Mode must be on.

Which devices and iOS versions work?

An iPad or iPhone on iOS or iPadOS 17.4 or later.

Can I use a controller?

Yes. DualSense, Xbox and MFi controllers work, and there is an on-screen GamePad for touch.

How do I turn JIT on?

In Settings, JIT: import your pairing file and connect LocalDevVPN. JIT starts on its own when you launch a game.

Can I import updates and DLC?

Yes. Updates and DLC can be imported for your games.

Is it free and open source?

Yes. EspressU is free and open source under MPL-2.0, and the source is on GitHub.

Is it affiliated with Nintendo?

No. EspressU is not affiliated with Nintendo. Wii U is a trademark of Nintendo.

Where did EspressU come from?

EspressU is built on Cemu and based on MuffinEMU by Void. See the About page for credits.

Where do I report a bug?

In the Discord (the bugs forum) or as an issue on GitHub. Say your device, iOS version and the game.
